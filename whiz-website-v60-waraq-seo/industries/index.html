Industry 01

Education

Universities, schools and training organisations run on many disconnected systems, from admissions and student information to learning platforms, finance and the public website. Whiz Solutions builds connected education environments covering learning management systems, education ERP modules, institutional analytics, Umbraco university websites and secure API integration, with MoHESR integration readiness for UAE higher-education institutions.

Solutions we deliver

  • MoHESR integration-readiness assessment and secure integration design for your SIS and institutional systems
  • Learning Management Systems with courses, live classes, assessments and progress tracking
  • Education ERP modules covering admissions, fees, examinations, timetables and multi-campus reporting
  • Institutional analytics dashboards for enrolment, attendance, progression and KPIs
  • University websites on Umbraco CMS with multilingual publishing and admissions integration
  • AI-assisted learning tools for content structuring, assessment generation and skill-gap analysis
Proof

Our team previously provided Umbraco CMS maintenance and support for the American University in Dubai (engagement concluded, and the university now manages its site internally), and we help UAE institutions assess their readiness for MoHESR electronic data integration.

Industry 02

Pharmaceutical & Life Sciences

Pharmaceutical and life sciences organizations operate under intense scrutiny, where every document, approval, and change must be traceable and defensible in an audit. Manual, paper-based approvals and scattered records create compliance risk and slow the business down. Whiz Solutions builds quality-driven digital systems with document control, electronic approvals, and complete audit trails at their core, designed with awareness of GxP, ALCOA+, Good Documentation Practices, and 21 CFR Part 11 principles.

Solutions we deliver

  • SharePoint Document Management Systems with metadata classification, version control, and controlled document libraries
  • Multi-level electronic approval workflows for purchasing, travel, and quality processes
  • Audit trails and activity logging that record who did what, when, and what changed
  • Electronic signatures and controlled document review, approval, and retention
  • Purchase order and requisition governance with full traceability
  • Power BI dashboards for operational and compliance visibility
Proof

For a pharmaceutical distribution company, we digitized paper-based purchase requisition and employee travel approvals on the Microsoft Power Platform, replacing email chains with multi-level workflows, mobile sign-off, notifications, and a complete, audit-ready trail.

Industry 03

Healthcare & Retail Pharmacy

Pharmacies, clinics, and healthcare providers generate enormous volumes of sales, inventory, and operational data across multiple branches and systems, but rarely have a single, trusted view of it. Slow, manual Excel reporting delays decisions on stock, purchasing, and branch performance. Whiz Solutions consolidates fragmented data into real-time dashboards and builds the operational systems healthcare businesses rely on.

Solutions we deliver

  • Power BI executive dashboards consolidating sales, inventory, purchasing, and branch data
  • KPI monitoring and drill-down analytics for management
  • Data consolidation across multiple source systems
  • Inventory movement and supplier performance reporting
  • Custom operational applications and reporting portals
Proof

For a regional pharmacy retail chain, we built interactive Power BI dashboards that unified sales, inventory, purchasing, and branch data from multiple systems into a single executive reporting platform, replacing slow, manual Excel reports with real-time KPI visibility.

Industry 04

Construction

Construction groups run multiple projects across dispersed sites, where daily progress, labour, and equipment data is captured on paper and reported days late. Management struggles to compare planned versus actual productivity, and critical documents are scattered across locations and versions. Whiz Solutions builds cloud-based platforms that capture site data in real time and centralize document control across every project and company.

Solutions we deliver

  • Cloud productivity monitoring with daily site logging from mobile devices
  • Planned-vs-actual variance reporting across labour, equipment, and completed quantities
  • Workforce time and attendance management across multiple sites
  • SharePoint-based enterprise document management with approval workflows and audit logs
  • BOQ integration, activity monitoring, and progress photo capture
  • Service request platforms for on-site logistics such as crane, water, and labour transport booking
Proof

For a leading Dubai construction group, we developed a cloud-based productivity monitoring platform that lets supervisors log labour, equipment, and completed quantities from the field, giving management live variance reporting across every project.

Industry 05

Automotive

Automotive sales and marketing operations depend on field teams capturing vehicle and customer data accurately across many dealerships, a process that breaks down when it runs on manual Excel sheets emailed back and forth. Duplicate VINs, manual registration data entry, and no live visibility slow reporting and cloud decisions. Whiz Solutions builds mobile-first sales automation with OCR and real-time dashboards.

Solutions we deliver

  • Flutter mobile applications for field data capture (Android & iOS)
  • OCR-powered vehicle registration certificate processing and data extraction
  • VIN validation and duplicate detection
  • Dealer approval workflows and role-based portals
  • Real-time management dashboards for sales, dealer, and trader performance
  • Multilingual (English & Arabic) support and cloud-hosted architecture
Proof

For a global automotive brand's marketing partner, we built an end-to-end sales automation platform that replaced manual Excel reporting across multiple dealerships, with a Flutter mobile app for field capture, OCR that automatically extracts registration details, and live dashboards for management.

Industry 06

Aviation

Aviation organizations run complex, high-volume processes across many countries, where policies, approvals, and compliance data must stay accurate and visible at global scale. When that work lives in spreadsheets and email approvals, tracking premiums, endorsements, and expiries across regions becomes slow and error-prone. Whiz Solutions builds Microsoft cloud platforms that consolidate multi-country operations into one governed system with live dashboards and controlled approvals.

Solutions we deliver

  • Microsoft cloud platforms for multi-country policy, approval, and compliance management
  • Approval workflows for policies, payments, invoices, and endorsements
  • Multi-country dashboards for expiring policies, premiums due, and pending claims
Proof

For an international airline, we built a medical insurance management system that streamlines the full insurance lifecycle across 40 countries, covering policy management, approvals, invoices, endorsements, premium tracking, and multi-country dashboards. It runs on SharePoint and the Power Platform, integrated with SAP SuccessFactors.

  • Workflow automation and multi-level approval systems
  • Field mobile applications connected to back-office systems
  • Power BI dashboards consolidating sales, inventory, and purchasing data
  • Role-based access and audit-ready record keeping
Proof

Across our pharmaceutical distribution and pharmacy retail engagements, we've digitized purchasing approvals and unified multi-system sales, inventory, and purchasing data into executive dashboards. These are the same patterns that drive trading and distribution operations.

Industry 09

Transportation & Logistics

Transportation and logistics operations lose time and money to manual booking, double-booked vehicles, and no central view of availability. Whether it's an internal fleet or a customer-facing booking platform, coordination happens over phone calls, WhatsApp, and spreadsheets. Whiz Solutions builds fleet booking, dispatch, and trip management platforms that bring the whole operation into one system.

Solutions we deliver

  • Vehicle booking and allocation platforms with availability calendars
  • Booking approval workflows and manual dispatch tools
  • Driver and vehicle management, including document and renewal tracking
  • Trip scheduling, mobile driver workflows, and real-time tracking
  • Utilization and operational reporting dashboards
Proof

We developed a centralized internal vehicle booking and dispatch platform for a UAE transport services company, replacing manual phone, WhatsApp, and spreadsheet allocation with online booking, driver assignment, an availability calendar, and utilization reporting. We also provided ongoing enhancement and support for a UAE truck-on-demand platform, contributing to the mobile apps and portal behind its customer booking and tracking experience.

Professional Services & Training

Professional services, training, and certification organizations handle high volumes of documents, approvals, and credentials, often prepared manually, one at a time, with slow and unreliable verification. Whiz Solutions automates certificate generation, approval workflows, and Microsoft 365 collaboration so these organizations can operate at scale with confidence.

Solutions we deliver

  • Automated certificate generation with personalized, branded templates
  • QR-code verification and digital signatures for instant authenticity checks
  • Bulk generation, participant management, and secure download portals
  • Approval workflows and audit trails
  • Microsoft 365, SharePoint, and Teams collaboration and document management
Proof

For a training and certification organization, we built an automated certificate platform that generates thousands of personalized certificates in seconds, each carrying a QR code and digital signature for instant online verification and a full audit trail.
