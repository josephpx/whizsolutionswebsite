Our Story

From managed IT to digital transformation partner

Whiz Solutions was established in Dubai in 2012 as an IT services company, serving businesses across the UAE with dependable technology support, Microsoft cloud deployments, and infrastructure management.

As our clients grew, so did their challenges. The organizations we worked with didn't just need their systems kept running. They needed their operations rethought. Spreadsheets and email chains were holding back sales teams, construction sites, pharmacy chains, and distribution companies that had outgrown manual ways of working. So we evolved.

Over more than a decade, Whiz Solutions grew from a managed IT provider into a full digital transformation partner. Today we design and build custom business applications, Microsoft 365 and SharePoint solutions, AI-powered document processing, and Power BI business intelligence, while continuing to secure and support the technology underneath it all.

That combination is deliberate. We've delivered 80+ projects across the Middle East and the United States, spanning pharmaceutical compliance workflows, construction productivity platforms, automotive sales automation, and enterprise document management. We understand not only how to build software, but how to keep it running, secure, and evolving long after go-live.

How We Deliver

Local accountability, delivery at scale

Whiz Solutions combines UAE-based consulting and project management with an extended team of software architects, UI/UX designers, developers, cloud engineers, and quality assurance professionals across the UAE and India.

This model gives our clients the best of both worlds. Local accountability, discovery, and project ownership sit in Dubai, backed by the delivery capacity to build and support enterprise-grade solutions at scale. You have a single point of accountability throughout, with one team responsible for scoping, designing, building, and supporting your solution end to end.

We build on proven, enterprise-grade technology, leveraging tools and expertise from leading global platforms including Microsoft, Azure, AWS, and Oracle, and supported by a network of certified technology partners and specialists.
