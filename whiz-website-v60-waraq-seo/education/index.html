For UAE Universities

MoHESR Integration Readiness

MoHESR is expanding electronic data integration across UAE higher-education institutions, enabling more accurate, secure and timely exchange of institutional information.

We help universities assess their integration readiness and design secure connections between their existing SIS, LMS, ERP and other institutional platforms and authority-defined interfaces. Our services can cover data mapping, transformation, validation, testing, monitoring, documentation and technical handover.

Every engagement begins with a review of the institution's systems, available technical specifications and current onboarding requirements. Project scope and timelines are confirmed after this assessment. All integration work is designed with security and governance controls that support applicable institutional and regulatory requirements.
