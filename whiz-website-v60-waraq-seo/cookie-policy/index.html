Cookie Policy

This page explains what cookies and similar technologies we use, why we use them, and how you can change your choices at any time.

Talk to us

Last updated: 8 July 2026

This document is a plain-English template. It should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before you rely on it.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies (such as local storage) work in comparable ways. We group them into three categories: strictly necessary, analytics, and marketing.

2. Cookies we use

Necessary cookies are always on because the site cannot work without them. Analytics and marketing cookies are off by default and load only if you accept them.

CategoryConsentExamples
Strictly necessaryAlways activewhiz-consent (stores your cookie choice, 180 days); Cloudflare Turnstile (spam/abuse protection on the contact form).
Analytics (optional)Only with consentIf enabled: Google Analytics (_ga, _ga_*, _gid) and/or Microsoft Clarity (_clck, _clsk). Used to understand site usage.
Marketing (optional)Only with consentIf enabled: LinkedIn Insight (li_*, bcookie, lidc), tag manager, and lead/chat tools (e.g. HubSpot, Zoho SalesIQ). Used for campaign measurement and audiences.

3. How to change your choices

You can review or change your consent at any time. Use the button below (also available as “Cookie Settings” in the site footer) to reopen the preferences panel:

You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking necessary cookies may stop parts of the site from working.

4. Third-party providers

Depending on the choices you make, cookies may be set by:

  • Cloudflare: Turnstile security (necessary) and optional cookieless web analytics.
  • Google: Google Analytics / Tag Manager (analytics/marketing), if enabled.
  • Microsoft: Clarity analytics, if enabled.
  • LinkedIn: Insight Tag marketing, if enabled.
