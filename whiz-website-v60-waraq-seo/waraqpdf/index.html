The important questions

Answers, without the fine print.

Click a question to see the complete answer. We explain the limits as clearly as the benefits.

Do you upload my files?

No. 20 of the 23 tools run entirely in your browser, so the file never leaves your device. The two Office conversion tools use a secure conversion engine, and we label them clearly.

Is the password protection real?

Yes. Password protection uses AES-256 encryption, the same standard used by professional PDF software, and it is applied on your own device.

Can you recover a forgotten PDF password?

No — and be careful with anyone who claims they can. Properly encrypted PDFs cannot be cracked. WaraqPDF’s Unlock tool removes a password you already know.

What’s the difference between Erase and Redact?

Erase covers content visually, like correction fluid. Redact permanently destroys the content underneath, which is required when the information must be unrecoverable.

Does OCR support Arabic?

Yes. OCR supports English, Arabic, or both together, and recognition runs on your device.

What do I need to install?

Nothing. WaraqPDF runs in any modern browser. Desktop apps for Windows and Mac are coming for teams that prefer them.
