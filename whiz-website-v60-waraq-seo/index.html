Automotive Sales Automation

Problem: Showroom teams re-typed vehicle registration details into Excel for every sale.

Built: A mobile sales platform where OCR reads the registration card and fills the deal automatically.

No more manual Excel. Data is captured in the field with validation at entry, so re-keying errors drop sharply.
