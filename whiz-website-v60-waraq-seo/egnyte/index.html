First, What Is Egnyte?

One secure home for every company file

Egnyte is a secure cloud platform where all of your company's files live. It looks and feels like the drives your team already uses, but it works from any device, online or offline, controls exactly who can see what, and has AI built in that can search, summarize and answer questions from your own documents. Over 17,000 organizations run their files on it. Whiz Solutions brings it to the UAE, deployed, migrated and managed for you.

Collaboration & Your Files

Work together in the tools you already use

Egnyte doesn't ask your team to change how they work. Designers stay in AutoCAD and Revit, editors stay in Premiere, accountants stay in Excel, and files simply open from a normal drive letter. What changes is everything around them. No more overwritten drawings, no more "final_v7_JOSEPH" copies flying around by email, and no more waiting an hour for a big file to upload.

📝

Real co-editing in Microsoft Office

Two or more people can edit the same Word, Excel or PowerPoint file at the same time, in the desktop apps or the browser, and see each other’s changes live. Google Workspace co-editing is supported the same way. No emailing copies back and forth.

🔒

Automatic file locking for every file type

The moment someone opens a file, it locks for everyone else, and unlocks when they close it. Crucially, this covers ALL formats, including CAD drawings, where generic platforms fall short. Two engineers can never silently overwrite each other’s work again.

📐

Built for AutoCAD, Revit & big design files

Egnyte understands how design applications behave. External references (XREFs) and linked models in AutoCAD, Revit, Rhino and SketchUp keep working exactly as they do on a local server. Files open straight from the mapped drive at desktop speed, with no VPN.

⚡

Delta sync means only changes travel

Save a 900 MB drawing and Egnyte uploads only the part that changed, not the whole file. Colleagues can even start downloading before a sync completes. That’s what makes cloud work practical on UAE site connections.

🔗

Sharing you can control

Send links instead of attachments, with passwords, expiry dates and download controls. Give subcontractors and clients access to exactly one folder, and collect files from them with upload-request links. Everything they touch is logged.

🕘

Every version, kept automatically

Each save becomes a version you can open or restore. A drawing overwritten by mistake, a spreadsheet ruined by a bad edit, even a file encrypted by ransomware are all recoverable in a few clicks, with a record of who changed what, when.

The Document Lifecycle

Every document has a life, from origin to archival

A document isn't just created and forgotten. It's born, worked on, approved, kept for as long as the law or your industry requires, archived, and finally disposed of, with proof. Most companies manage the first half well and ignore the second half completely. Files pile up forever (cost and legal risk), or worse, records that must be kept get deleted. Egnyte manages the entire lifecycle with automatic policies, and Whiz sets them up around your legal and industry requirements.

01📄

Create & Capture

A document is born, whether uploaded, scanned, or saved from an app. AI applies metadata immediately (project, type, department), so it starts life classified, not lost in a folder.

02🤝

Collaborate

The working years bring co-editing, versions, file locking and controlled sharing with partners. Every change tracked, every version kept.

03✅

Approve & Control

The document becomes a record once it is reviewed, approved and e-signed (21 CFR Part 11 in pharma). From here it is controlled, and the effective version is unambiguous.

the forgotten half
04🗄️

Retain

A retention policy attaches automatically, based on what the document is, whether contract, batch record, SOP or project file. While retention runs, the record cannot be quietly deleted, even by an admin.

the forgotten half
05📦

Archive

When its active life ends, the document moves to archive storage automatically, out of everyday view and cheaper to keep, but instantly retrievable for an audit, a dispute, or a warranty claim.

the forgotten half
06🧹

Dispose defensibly

When retention expires, the policy archives, deletes, or flags the file for review, and records that it did. You can prove not only what you kept, but why what was deleted was allowed to go.

Most companies stop at stage 3

Everything ever created sits on the server forever. Nobody knows what can be deleted, storage and backups keep growing, and old files with personal data become a silent liability. Lifecycle policies fix this permanently. Set once, they are enforced automatically, and no one has to remember anything.

🧪 In pharma, this isn't optional

GxP expects defined retention for batch records, SOPs and quality documents, and defensible destruction when it ends. An auditor won't just ask for your retention SOP; they'll ask to see it executed. With lifecycle policies, the execution log is the audit evidence.

Egnyte + SharePoint

Already on SharePoint? You don't have to choose

This is the question we hear most, and the answer is yes, they work together natively. Depending on what you need, Egnyte can live inside your SharePoint, govern it from above, or help you move off it entirely. As a Microsoft house and an Egnyte partner, we architect whichever path fits, and we tell you honestly which one that is.

🧩

Coexist with Egnyte inside SharePoint

The Egnyte web part embeds your Egnyte folders directly into SharePoint pages, so users view and manage files without leaving SharePoint, and Egnyte permissions stay enforced. Office co-editing works natively, including SharePoint Embedded co-editing with @mentions and Microsoft sensitivity labels.

🛡️

Govern with one control panel over Microsoft 365

Egnyte scans your SharePoint and OneDrive for sensitive information such as patient data, personal IDs and contracts, then applies classification, retention and archival policies across the whole Microsoft estate. Nothing moves. Everything becomes governed. For regulated industries, this is audit-readiness without a migration project.

🚚

Migrate natively, when you’re ready

Egnyte includes a built-in SharePoint Online migration tool that maps site libraries to Egnyte folders, preserves permissions, and tracks progress from the console, with no third-party migration software. Whiz plans the structure, runs the move, and retrains your team.

🧪 Why pharma teams care: quality documents often live in SharePoint with no defensible retention or sensitive-data control. Egnyte's governance layer fixes that without disrupting how your teams work today. Then, if you choose, controlled documents graduate to Egnyte's validated, Part 11 environment at your pace.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

We already use SharePoint. Can Egnyte integrate with it?
Yes, natively, in three ways. You can embed Egnyte folders directly inside SharePoint pages with the Egnyte web part (users never leave SharePoint, Egnyte permissions stay enforced). You can use Egnyte as a governance layer that scans SharePoint and OneDrive for sensitive data and applies retention and archival policies across Microsoft 365 without moving anything. Or you can migrate from SharePoint to Egnyte with Egnyte’s built-in migration tool, which maps folders, preserves permissions and tracks progress. Many of our customers run both platforms; as the partner managing your Microsoft environment, we advise honestly which content belongs where.
What does Whiz Solutions actually provide as an Egnyte MSP?
Licensing, deployment, data migration, permission and governance design, integration with your existing systems (Microsoft 365, Procore, and custom applications we build), user training, and ongoing administration and support under one managed agreement, with a local team in Dubai.
Is the AI safe to use on confidential documents?
Egnyte’s AI works inside your governed repository and respects existing permissions, so users only get answers from files they already have access to, with sources cited. It also includes sensitive-data detection to flag and remediate exposure. We configure these controls as part of every rollout.
Can Egnyte replace our file server, and does it work with AutoCAD, DocuSign and Adobe Sign?
Yes. Egnyte is a full cloud file server replacement, with mapped drive letters at desktop speed with no VPN, automatic file locking for every format including AutoCAD and Revit (where SharePoint and generic drives struggle), and offline access in the field. For approvals, documents can be routed and signed without leaving the platform using Egnyte Sign, Adobe Acrobat Sign or DocuSign, and the signed copy returns to the folder automatically, on the audit trail.
