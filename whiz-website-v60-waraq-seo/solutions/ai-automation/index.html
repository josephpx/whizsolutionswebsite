AI & Intelligent Automation

AI & Intelligent Automation Solutions in Dubai

Whiz Solutions applies practical AI where it delivers measurable value, such as extracting data from documents, validating information, and removing repetitive manual work. Our AI document processing and OCR solutions convert paperwork, images, and scanned records into structured, accurate data your systems can use. We integrate Azure AI, Azure OCR, and OpenAI into real business workflows, so AI does useful work inside your operations rather than sitting in a demo.

What We Deliver

Capabilities built around your operations

AI Document Processing & OCR

We build OCR solutions that read scanned documents, images, and forms and turn them into structured, usable data. Using Azure OCR combined with AI-assisted extraction, we capture fields like reference numbers, IDs, and line items automatically instead of having staff retype them.

Intelligent Data Extraction & Validation

We extract specific data points from documents and validate them against rules and existing records before they enter your systems. Automated checks catch duplicates, malformed values, and mismatches at the point of capture, raising accuracy and cutting rework.

Azure AI Integration

We integrate Azure AI services, including OCR, document intelligence, and cognitive capabilities, into custom applications and workflows. AI becomes a component of your business system, processing documents and data as part of the normal flow.

OpenAI & LLM Integration

We integrate OpenAI and large language models into business applications for tasks like summarization, classification, drafting, and natural-language querying of your own content. We scope these carefully so the model is used where it's reliable and adds real value.

Knowledge Management & AI Search

We build knowledge management solutions where staff can ask questions in natural language and get answers grounded in your own documents and data. This turns scattered policies, procedures, and records into a system people can actually query.

Document Classification & Verification

We automate sorting and verification of incoming documents, classifying by type, routing to the right workflow, and flagging exceptions for review. High-volume document handling becomes a controlled, automated pipeline.

Business Process Automation

We combine AI extraction with workflow automation so an entire process runs with minimal manual touch, from capture and validation through routing, approval, and record keeping. AI handles the reading and checking. Automation handles the movement and sign-off.

AI-Assisted Reporting & Insights

We layer AI on top of your data to surface patterns, summaries, and exceptions that would take analysts hours to compile. Management gets faster insight without expanding the reporting team.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can AI extract data from scanned documents and images?
Yes. We build OCR and AI document processing that read scanned documents, photos, and forms and extract specific fields such as reference numbers, IDs, and line items into structured data your systems can use. We validate the extracted data before it's committed, so accuracy is checked rather than assumed.
Do you integrate OpenAI / ChatGPT into business applications?
Yes. We integrate OpenAI and large language models into custom applications for summarization, classification, drafting, and natural-language search over your own content. We scope each use case to where the model is reliable, with guardrails and validation so it fits into a real business process.
How accurate is OCR data extraction?
Accuracy depends on document quality and type, which is why we prototype on your real documents first and add validation rules and human review where it matters. In practice this replaces slow manual typing with automated capture that is both faster and more consistent, with errors caught at the point of entry.
What's the difference between OCR and AI document processing?
OCR reads text from images; AI document processing goes further, understanding document structure, extracting specific fields, classifying document types, and validating the results. We combine both so you get not just recognized text but structured, verified data ready for your systems.
Where does AI actually save us money?
The clearest returns come from document- and data-heavy processes such as registration capture, invoice and form processing, data validation, and knowledge search. We identify the specific process where AI cuts manual effort and error, prototype it, and prove the gain before a full build.
