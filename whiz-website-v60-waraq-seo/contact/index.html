FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with clients outside the UAE?
Yes. While we're headquartered in Dubai, we've delivered projects across the Middle East and the United States. We work comfortably with remote clients and coordinate across time zones, so your location isn't a barrier to working with us.
Do you offer AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) support?
Yes. Alongside project delivery, we provide managed IT services and ongoing AMC support, including Microsoft 365 administration, endpoint management, backup, security, and helpdesk support, so your systems stay secure, updated, and running well after go-live.
What's a typical project timeline?
It depends on scope. A focused workflow automation or dashboard project can be delivered in a few weeks, while a larger custom platform or enterprise SharePoint rollout runs over several months. After our discovery call, we'll give you a clear, realistic timeline as part of your proposal.
